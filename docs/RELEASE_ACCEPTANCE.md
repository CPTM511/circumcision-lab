## Interpretation

Visual realism, mechanical fidelity and clinical validity remain separate. Original procedural anatomy has visible anatomical detail; materials are not calibrated, contact is sampled and high-strain crumpling remains. Passive authored edge recoil and synthetic attachments do not demonstrate surgical suturing. There is no bleeding, perfusion, healing, patient-use or clinical-validation claim. Passing three declared synthetic cases does not establish generalization or complete operative competence.

The HTML media preserves actual observation frames with original timestamps, at accelerated playback speed. It is not continuous screen video, interpolated footage or a newly executed model episode.
